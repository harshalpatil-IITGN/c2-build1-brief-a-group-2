## Parts we never had to choose about

1. **Patient Identifier Normalization**: Patient IDs (`PAHM0000001`, `PBLR0000001`, etc.) were structurally identical across legacy and Nova systems (4-letter city code followed by 7 digits). No complex identity merging was required for patient matching.
2. **Channel Assignment Logic**: For rows with matched bookings, the channel classification was strictly binary (`CLINIC` vs `HOME`), requiring no heuristic clustering.
