## The entry we are proudest of

**Challenge 1 & 7 · Unraveling the Nova Software Migration in Jaipur and Chennai**:
When we first loaded the 2026 bookings for Jaipur and Chennai, test codes were missing on over 26,000 rows, and the apparent laboratory fulfilment rate plummeted to 0.77. It appeared as though the new software was corrupting records and losing lab tests. 

Instead of accepting this at face value or discarding the data, we parsed the nested JSON arrays, expanded tests into discrete rows, and investigated booking statuses. We discovered that Nova created new booking IDs for rescheduled visits without deleting the old ones. Once we filtered out the 1,500 rescheduled duplicate IDs that never received tests, the lab fulfilment rate across Jaipur and Chennai immediately normalized to 82.5%–83.3%—perfectly matching the other four legacy cities. Furthermore, by proving that Jaipur and Chennai were already underperforming in January 2026 (three months before Nova launched), we prevented leadership from mistakenly blaming their IT systems, directing their focus instead to real patient acquisition shortfalls.
