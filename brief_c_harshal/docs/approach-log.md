## Parts we never had to choose about

- **File format**: invoices and payments are both CSV from the data pack. No choice was needed on parsing format (versus JSON or Parquet).
- **Currency**: All amounts are in INR. No FX conversion required.
- **Period**: The brief specifies Q1 and Q2 2026. Start and end dates are fixed by the data filenames.
